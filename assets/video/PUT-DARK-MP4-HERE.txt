Put your video in THIS folder (assets/video/). Recommended name: dark.mp4

The site also accepts these names, so a Windows "hidden extension" mistake still works:
  dark.mp4   dark.mp4.mp4   dark.MP4   dark.webm   dark.mov   dark.m4v

The first one that exists and can be played is shown in the About photo
(muted, looping). If none plays, the photo stays.

Best format: H.264 MP4. Phone/screen-recorder files are often H.265 (HEVC),
which Chrome/Firefox cannot play. Convert with:
  ffmpeg -i input.mp4 -c:v libx264 -profile:v high -pix_fmt yuv420p -an -movflags +faststart -vf scale=-2:720 dark.mp4
After adding the file, hard refresh (Ctrl+F5).
